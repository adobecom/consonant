Contract-driven design systems

where AI and design systems are converging — and why parity is the proof

A description informs. A contract arbitrates.

  • One machine-readable contract per component: props, anatomy, tokens, behaviour — derived from what the design file actually says.
  • Figma and code are both measured against it. Neither is the source of truth; the contract is, and the evidence behind it is observable.
  • Agents work inside that loop: extract, draft, refuse, heal, prove. No step is a black box, because every step leaves a file.
  • The community is converging on the same shape (Southleft's contract playground, the design-system contract schemas). Ours emits that schema.
