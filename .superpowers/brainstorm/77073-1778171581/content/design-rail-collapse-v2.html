Pick a label word for the collapse trigger

Used next to the ‹ and › chevrons. Should feel reversible — not permanent.

A

Hide

Simple, clean, universally understood. Implies "it's still there, just not visible." Familiar from most tools.

B

Collapse

Standard panel terminology. Used in VS Code, Figma, and most IDEs. Most precise for what it does.

C

Minimize

Borrowed from OS window management. Feels very familiar but implies shrinking, not sliding away.

D

Fold

Directional and spatial — the panel "folds" to the edge. Unique, a little playful, still intuitive.
