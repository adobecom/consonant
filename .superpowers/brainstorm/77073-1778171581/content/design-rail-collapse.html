Behavior Notes

Collapse trigger
  • Arrow button inside rail (bottom center of rail)
  • Slides rail out with CSS transition (200ms ease)
Expand trigger
  • Click the circular FAB icon (lower-left or lower-right of browser area)
  • Rail slides back in from the edge
FAB icons
  • Left FAB: ☰ (extract panel)
  • Right FAB: 💬 (Claude chat)
  • Purple, 40px, floating above browser content
Persistence
  • Rail state saved in chrome.storage.local
  • Remembered across sessions per user
